# Northstar Electronics — Shipping Policy

This is fictional policy content created for the ResolveAI demo.

## Shipping destinations

Northstar Electronics ships to the 48 contiguous United States.
Shipping to Alaska, Hawaii, and international destinations is not available.

## Order processing

Orders are processed within two business days.
Orders are not processed on weekends or company holidays.

## Standard shipping

Standard shipping takes three to five business days after dispatch.
It is free for orders totaling $75 or more before tax.
For orders below $75, standard shipping costs $6.99.

## Express shipping

Express shipping costs $14.99 and takes one to two business days
after dispatch. Order processing time still applies.

## Tracking

Customers receive a tracking link by email when their order ships.
Tracking updates may take up to 24 hours to appear.

## Delayed deliveries

If an order has not arrived within three business days after its
estimated delivery date, customers should contact support with their
order number.

## Packages marked delivered

If tracking shows delivered but the package cannot be found, customers
should check nearby delivery locations and with household members.

If the package is still missing after 24 hours, customers should contact
support. A refund or replacement is not guaranteed before investigation.
