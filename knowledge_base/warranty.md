# Northstar Electronics — Warranty Policy

This is fictional policy content created for the ResolveAI demo.

## Coverage period

Products sold by Northstar Electronics include a one-year limited
warranty beginning on the delivery date.

## Covered defects

The warranty covers defects in materials or workmanship under normal use.

## Exclusions

The warranty does not cover accidental damage, liquid damage, misuse,
unauthorized repairs, or normal wear and tear.

## Starting a claim

Customers must contact support with their order number, a description
of the defect, and photos or video showing the issue when possible.

Proof of purchase is required.

## Claim resolution

After reviewing a claim, Northstar Electronics may repair the product
or replace it with an equivalent product.

A refund is not guaranteed under the warranty.

## Warranty shipping

Northstar Electronics pays shipping costs for approved warranty claims
within its supported shipping destinations.

## Relationship to returns

The warranty is separate from the 30-day return policy.
A product outside the return window may still qualify for warranty
service if it is within the one-year warranty period.
